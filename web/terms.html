What the Service is

AppMates is two things bundled together: a set of pre-flight checks that read public App Store and Google Play catalogue data (no account required), and a testing marketplace where developers exchange closed testing and honest feedback with each other.

The marketplace: what it is and isn't

  • Testing slots are earned by testing other people's apps, not purchased — there is currently no real-money transaction anywhere in the marketplace.
  • Tokens have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, transferred for money, or redeemed outside AppMates.

What you exchange here is private feedback on a build and, where relevant, a closed-testing invite.
  • Closed testing itself is invitation-only on both stores regardless of AppMates — the marketplace helps you find who to invite, it doesn't change what either store requires.
