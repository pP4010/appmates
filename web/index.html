How a tester gets earned

  1. 1 You test someone else's build Ask for a slot with a short pitch. The developer reviews it and accepts or declines — closed testing is invitation-only on both stores anyway.
  2. 2 They confirm you actually helped You send private feedback. Only the listing's owner can mark it complete, and only that mints a token. Nobody can credit themselves.
  3. 3 You spend it on your own listing Tokens buy visibility for your app — the same kind of placement a launch directory sells, except this one is earned.
What this isn't. Nobody here trades App Store or Play reviews, and nothing on this site ever asks a tester to leave one. Both stores ban incentivised reviews outright, and a developer account is not worth risking over a placement. Testers give you private feedback on a closed build; whether anyone reviews your app is entirely between them and the store. Tokens cannot be bought — not at any price, not ever.
