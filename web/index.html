Repair

Flattens transparency onto your chosen background, converts to sRGB and letterboxes to the nearest valid resolution — never stretching, since a distorted UI screenshot reads as broken to reviewers.

Output is JPEG, and that is deliberate: a canvas-exported PNG still carries a 32-bit alpha channel even when every pixel is opaque, so it would fail the very rule you came here to fix. JPEG cannot represent alpha at all. Both stores accept it. The command-line tool writes real alpha-free PNGs if you need PNG specifically.

Apple indexes your app name, subtitle and the 100-character keyword field as one pool of words, then combines them to form matches. So a phrase is reachable when each of its words is somewhere in the pool — the phrase itself never has to appear — a word already in your title is already indexed, and every space is overhead.

The field is never shown to a user, so none of this is self-correcting: a listing wasting a third of its budget looks exactly like a perfect one.
